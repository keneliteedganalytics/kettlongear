## Screen applied
- Categories kept: Kitchen and Dining, Tools and Home Improvement, Pet Supplies, Automotive. Dropped: Health and Household (53), Toys and Games (38), Beauty (24) as gated or compliance-heavy.
- Seller country USA only (a US-based third-party seller signals a distributor channel exists).
- No Frequently Returned Item badge.
- Max buy cost = (price x 0.85 referral net) minus $5.75 estimated FBA fulfillment minus $1.00 prep/inbound, divided by 1.25 for a 25 percent ROI floor. Verify each with Amazon's Revenue Calculator before ordering.
