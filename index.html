Fit guide

Find your size in ten seconds

Wrap a soft tape around your forearm 3 inches (about 8 cm) below the wrist bone, snug but not tight. Match the number below. Between sizes, size down for a firmer hold.

6 to 7.5 in
15 to 19 cm
M

19 to 23 cm
